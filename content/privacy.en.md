### 4. Managing and Rejecting Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your local drive by your web browser. You retain full control over cookie permissions in browser settings:

* **Google Chrome**: Settings ➔ Privacy and security ➔ Third-party cookies ➔ Block third-party cookies
* **Microsoft Edge**: Settings ➔ Cookies and site permissions ➔ Manage and delete cookies and site data
* **Apple Safari**: Settings ➔ Privacy ➔ Block all cookies

*(Note: Completely blocking cookies may affect certain interactive site features.)*
